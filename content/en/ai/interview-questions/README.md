Interviews for AI application development rarely stop at just asking "what is concept X." More commonly, interviewers drill down through a project: why it was designed that way, how issues were debugged and resolved, how it was evaluated after launch, and how costs and security were managed.

This **AI Application Development Interview Questions** guide is designed for AI engineers, AI application developers, and backend engineers transitioning into AI roles. It organizes core topics—"LLM Fundamentals, AI Agents, RAG, and AI System Design"—into a coherent, structured review roadmap.

## Who Is This For?

- Candidates preparing for interviews in AI application development, AI engineering, or backend-to-AI transition roles.
- Readers who understand basic AI concepts but tend to give unfocused, superficial answers, or who remain stuck at the demo level during interviews.
- Developers looking to articulate project experiences using a structured narrative: "Problem -> Principles -> Solution -> Trade-offs -> Production Delivery."

## Core Focus Areas

- **LLM Fundamentals:** Clearly explain Tokens, context windows, sampling parameters, structured outputs, model invocation chains, and evaluation.
- **AI Agents:** Clearly explain Agent Loops, Memory, Prompts, Context, MCP, Skills, and workflows.
- **RAG:** Clearly explain document processing, vector retrieval, hybrid search, Reranking, GraphRAG, knowledge base updates, and evaluation.
- **AI System Design:** Clearly explain model gateways, observability, cost control, safety/guardrails, canary deployments, and real-time voice Agents.

## High-Frequency Questions

- When the interviewer asks, "Have you built an AI application?", how do you structure your answer across business scenarios, architecture, evaluation, and production governance?
- Why do LLM API calls require retries, rate limiting, fallbacks, and schema validation?
- What is the difference between an AI Agent and a standard deterministic workflow?
- When a RAG system fails to retrieve, retrieves the wrong context, or generates incorrect answers, how do you debug each issue?
- How does AI application system design address stability, observability, cost control, and security guardrails?
